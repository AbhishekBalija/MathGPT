# How Indian schools and colleges expect math answers to be written

Research for GitHub issue #38. Goal: show NeoMath solutions in the format students are marked on.

Rules for this document: every claim has a source link. If I could not confirm something, it says "not confirmed". Many official PDFs could not be opened by the research tools (403 or unreadable), and I cannot view images, so claims about handwritten sheets come from descriptions, not from the sheets. Those are labelled "description".

## Summary

- CBSE uses step marking. Marks go to the working (formula, substitution, calculation, conclusion), not only the final answer. Alternative methods get proportional marks. Source: [CBSE Class X marking scheme 2024-25 (copy hosted on esaral.com)](https://www.esaral.com/media/uploads/2025/12/1/12348-X_MS_041_Mathematics%20Standard_30-1-1_2024-25.pdf), and a search summary of the 2015 CBSE schemes (see "Official marking schemes").
- The final answer goes at the end, as a plain sentence with units ("Speed of train = 40 km/h", "Hence sides are 15 cm and 20 cm"). Same marking scheme.
- Longer answers start with "Given" and "To find / To prove" in teacher guidance ([EduRev guide](https://edurev.in/t/479159/Answer-Writing-Guide-for-Mathematics), third party). The official marking scheme itself shows "Let ..." and "ATQ" (According To the Question), and gives a mark for "Correct figure" in geometry.
- Primary long division in India uses the same layout as English-speaking countries ([Wikipedia: Long division](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_division) says India uses the same notation as English-speaking nations). So no special "Indian layout" is needed. The old claim that India differs is NOT confirmed.
- At university, proofs are written as sentences ("Proof." ... "we") and answers to calculus problems need supporting work ([UW proof-writing handout](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~lee/Courses/444-2009/writing-proofs.pdf), [AP Calculus exam tips](https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-calculus-bc/exam-tips)). Indian university marking rules: not confirmed.
- Math textbooks in Hindi and regional languages mostly use Western digits for numbers. Balbharati (Maharashtra) shows both Devanagari and international digits in class 5 ([shaalaa Balbharati class 5](https://www.shaalaa.com/mar/textbook-solutions/c/balbharati-solutions-mathematics-english-standard-5-maharashtra-state-board-chapter-2-number-work_3399)). Wider rules: not confirmed.

## Official marking schemes (what earns marks)

Evidence from the CBSE Class X Mathematics (Standard) 2024-25 marking scheme, paper 30/1/1 (copy at [esaral.com](https://www.esaral.com/media/uploads/2025/12/1/12348-X_MS_041_Mathematics%20Standard_30-1-1_2024-25.pdf)). I extracted the text myself. The maths symbols did not extract, but the mark splits did. Note: the file header says "Strictly Confidential, for internal use". It is hosted publicly by a third party, so treat the copy as unofficial and do not copy its text into the product.

General instructions in that file (paraphrased):
- Marking scheme carries "only suggested value points ... guidelines only", students may use their own expression and get due marks.
- "No marks to be deducted for the cumulative effect of an error. It should be penalized only once." (instruction 10). This is the rule behind "follow-through" marks: a wrong early value does not cost every later step.
- A different but correct expression gets marks. A search summary of the 2015 CBSE schemes (see [Delhi 2015](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/marking-scheme/2015/Mathematics_X_%20Delhi.pdf), could not open directly, 403) says "Alternative methods are accepted. Proportional marks are to be awarded." This is a search-engine quote, not read by me. Treat as probable.

Mark splits seen in the 2024-25 scheme (the question numbers refer to that paper):

| Question type | Example in scheme | Marks pattern I could read |
|---|---|---|
| Factorise a quadratic (2 marks) | Q22: split middle term, factor, zeroes | four steps of 1/2 each: split the term, group, factorise, write zeroes |
| Tangent / right triangle with units (2 marks) | Q25 | 1/2 radius-tangent angle = 90 degrees, 1 for Pythagoras with numbers, 1/2 answer "radius = 24 m" |
| Trig identity (3 marks) | Q27 | steps 1/2, 1, 1/2, 1/2, 1/2 down to "= RHS" |
| Proof by contradiction, irrational (3 marks) | Q29 | assume rational p/q coprime (1/2), square and show p divisible by 5 (1), q divisible by 5 (1), contradiction (1/2) |
| Quadratic word problem (5 marks) | Q34(a): sides of a right triangle | "Let the other two sides be x and y" (1), equation from the question "ATQ" (1/2), substitute (1), quadratic (1), solve (1), pick the valid roots and write "Hence sides are 15 cm and 20 cm" (1/2) |
| Quadratic word problem, train speed | Q34(b) | "Let the speed be x km/h", form equation (1 1/2), solve (1), "Speed of train = 40 km/h" (1/2) |
| Geometry proof (5 marks) | Q33 | "Correct figure" earns a mark, then each reason line earns 1 |
| Simultaneous equations (5 marks) | Q32 | form two equations (1, 1/2, 1, 1/2), solve (1), state answer (1) |
| Statistics (5 marks) | Q35 | build correct table (a mark), mean formula, solve for f, modal class, mode formula |
| Case study (4 marks, 3 parts) | Q36-38 | each sub-part has its own marks, with a small calculation each |

What this tells us: a mark is attached to specific kinds of lines, not to every line. See "What earns step marks" below.

Class 12 (calculus etc.): the search summary of the 2015 CBSE re-evaluation schemes says "In question(s) on differential equations, constant of integration has to be written." (via search, files returned 403: [Delhi](https://cbse.gov.in/curric~1/qpms2015/Re_Evalution_Class_XII_2015/Mathemaics/Mathematics_Delhi.pdf)). Per-step splits for class 12: not confirmed.

ICSE/ISC: CISCE publishes "Analysis of Pupil Performance" with marking scheme and examiner comments ([cisce.org Mathematics.pdf](https://cisce.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Mathematics.pdf), got 404 when fetched). I could not read any CISCE mark split. Not confirmed. Generic ICSE tips from [Oswaal](https://oswalpublishers.com/blog/answer-writing-tips-for-class-10-half-yearly-exams/) and others say "show all steps", units, box the answer. Those are tips, not CISCE rules.

## Topper sheets and teacher guidance (description only)

- CBSE publishes "Model answer by candidate" (topper sheets) under Examination > Exam Related Materials on cbse.gov.in; years listed 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024 and older; 2025 not released ([Careers360](https://school.careers360.com/articles/cbse-class-10-topper-answer-sheet)). I could not open or read the PDFs, so I cannot say anything first-hand about use of "therefore" signs, underlines or boxes in them. Not confirmed from the sheets.
- Description of the 2024 class 10 maths topper sheet by a coaching blog ([cbseguidanceweb](https://www.cbseguidanceweb.com/class-10-maths-toppers-answer-sheet-2024-pdf/)): given data first, formula before applying it, step-by-step calculation, neat pencil diagrams, final answer boxed, proper spacing, graph scale in a box, mistakes struck with a single line (the article points to Question 25). This is a blog description, not the sheet.
- Teacher/tutor guidance on structure: Given, To find/prove, Formula (boxed or underlined), Calculation without skipping, Final answer with units boxed ([EduRev answer-writing guide](https://edurev.in/t/479159/Answer-Writing-Guide-for-Mathematics); same list in a search summary of [aasoka](https://aasoka.com/blog/mistakes-in-board-exams/) and [Ecole Globale](https://www.ecoleglobale.com/blog/effective-answers-in-board-exams/)). Also: align successive equations vertically, keep exact values (root 2, pi) unless told to approximate.
- A formula alone does not earn marks, you must go on and use it ([PW: does CBSE give step marks](https://www.pw.live/school-prep/exams/does-cbse-give-step-marks-in-maths)). Step marks for correct formula, correct substitution, logical steps: same source.
- Math experts' tips: [smbceo](https://smbceo.com/2021/07/21/useful-tips-from-the-math-experts-to-get-90-marks-in-math-class-10-board-exams/): "you cannot just write the answer to get full marks".
- Construction questions need the written steps as well as the figure: not confirmed (the source page did not include the answer).

## Primary level (classes 1-5)

- Current NCERT books: Class 1-2 "Joyful Mathematics", class 3 onward "Maths Mela" (class 3 has 14 chapters, class 5 has 15), replacing the older "Math-Magic" ([CIET NCERT Maths Mela](https://ciet.ncert.gov.in/audio-book/158), [EduRev Maths Mela list](https://edurev.in/courses/82468_Mathematics-for-Class-3-Maths-Mela)). So "Math-Magic" in the brief is the older series.
- Class 3: division is introduced as fair sharing with objects, pictures, repeated subtraction and tables (chapter "Fair Share", [EduRev](https://edurev.in/t/349965/chapter-notes-fair-share)). The formal long division is taught in later classes (search summary; exact class and the NCERT page layout: not confirmed because I could not open the NCERT PDFs).
- Long division layout used in Indian-curriculum material: divisor to the left of the dividend, quotient written above it, steps Divide, Multiply, Subtract, Bring down, repeated; answer as quotient and remainder, check with Dividend = Divisor x Quotient + Remainder ([Vedantu class 3 long division](https://www.vedantu.com/maths/long-division-for-class-3), a tutoring site, not a board). Compared with the US layout (divisor outside a right bracket, quotient on an overbar, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_division)) the sources show no difference.
- Column addition/subtraction with carry/borrow and fractions layout in NCERT: not confirmed (could not open the books). Handwritten workbook examples: not confirmed.

| Item | Expected |
|---|---|

## Board differences

- CBSE: evidence above. ICSE/ISC: only generic tips found; official split not confirmed.
- Maharashtra: geometry paper needs a figure with the proof, theorem proofs commonly fill-in style in model papers (search result, [shaalaa Maharashtra geometry](https://www.shaalaa.com/mar/question-paper-solution/maharashtra-board-ssc-geometry-mathematics-2-10th-standard-2022-2023-official_18491)). - Tamil Nadu and Karnataka: no usable source found for answer layout or marks. Not confirmed.

## What earns step marks

Based on the CBSE paper above and PW/EduRev guidance.

| Question type | Steps that carry marks | How NeoMath should show them |
|---|---|---|
| Quadratic equation | Standard form or split middle term, factorisation, each root, rejecting an invalid root | Tag these steps "earns marks"; show the chosen method name |
| Word problem leading to an equation | "Let x be ..." with unit, equation from the question, solving, picking the valid answer, concluding sentence with unit | Separate "Let / Setup" step, "Solve" steps, and a final "Answer" sentence with unit |
| Formula-based (area, volume, trig heights) | Formula, substitution, result with unit | Show formula as its own line, then substitution, then result |
| Geometry proof | Correct figure, each statement with its reason | Statement and Reason per step, figure step first |
| Proof by contradiction (irrational) | Assumption, derived consequences, contradiction, conclusion | Labelled proof steps |
| Identity (trig) | LHS to RHS steps, "= RHS" | Chain of equalities ending with LHS = RHS |
| Statistics (mean, mode) | Correct table, formula, substitution, result | Table step, formula step, calculation |
| Calculus (class 12 / college) | Method steps; +C where needed; per-step splits not confirmed | Tag method steps; always show +C for indefinite integrals |

Rule of thumb from the sources: formula, substitution, calculation and final statement are the mark-carrying kinds. Arithmetic-only lines are not shown as separate marks in the scheme I read.

## Implications for NeoMath's design

These are recommendations, not sourced facts.

1. Answer-first vs answer-at-end. Boards expect the answer at the end, as a sentence. For chat, a short "answer" can be shown, but the written solution a student copies should end with the final answer line. Offer a "board style" view that ends with the answer, and keep the one-line answer as a summary.
2. Section headings. Use Given / To find (or To prove) for long answers (class 9 and up), skip them for 1-2 mark questions and for classes 1-5. Backed by teacher guides, not by the official scheme, so make it a per-level default.
3. Visual step types needed: long division, column addition/subtraction with carry, fraction layouts, a figure/diagram step for geometry, a table step for statistics, and a proof (statement plus reason) step. Without these, primary students cannot copy the working.
4. Method choice. Default to the method taught at that level (class 10: factorisation or formula; completing the square exists in the NCERT chapter). Let the student pick a method, since the CBSE scheme gives proportional marks for alternatives. Never use methods outside the syllabus (for example, calculus for a class 9 problem).
5. Step marks. Mark each step with a kind (setup, formula, substitution, calculation, reasoning, conclusion) and a flag "earns marks". Do not claim exact mark values, since splits differ per question.
6. Data model beyond a flat list of steps:
   - A solution header: level, board, language, question type.
   - Typed sections: given, to find, figure, steps, answer.
   - Step fields: kind, text/LaTeX, reason (for proofs), earnsMarks, optional visual (long-division grid, column layout, table).
   - Final answer object: value, unit, sentence form.
   - Method id and alternatives list.
   - Per-level display profile (which sections and how much arithmetic to show), so college output skips arithmetic.
